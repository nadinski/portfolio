Case 02 · Shar-Shara

Same owners.
Bigger stage.

A restaurant group — the whole guest system

Two restaurants, a fast-casual kebab spot and a banquet hall. Uzbek and European cuisine. Shar-shara means waterfall.

This time it wasn't one restaurant but a group — so we built a system instead of a website. Every venue gets its own QR menu, the banquet hall its own, and the print carries the same identity into the guest's hands. Then came the question that started everything: the aggregators were taking a cut of every order and keeping the guests. So we prototyped a delivery app the group would own outright — its own brand, its own customers, no commission.

Other projects

HGK Engineering

A site that
doesn't sell.
It vouches.

No lead form, no stock hero — every decision spent on proving precision

A structural-execution contractor working Istanbul to Dubai, whose clients arrive warm — by reputation, not by advert. So this one needed the opposite of a sales page.

No lead form. No pop-up. Nothing on the page asks for an email, because nobody buys a structural contractor from a banner — they check whether you look like you can be trusted with a building. So every decision went into proof of precision: a live structural wireframe instead of a stock hero, monospace type borrowed from drawings, and numbers that count themselves up rather than adjectives that claim.

Svarcom

A catalogue
that had to be
found fast.

Rebuilt so one cylinder is found in seconds, not clicks

Svarcom supplies technical, medical and food gases across St. Petersburg, and has since 1995. Their customers are welders, factories and hospitals: they don't browse — they need a specific gas today, and to know it will actually arrive.

So the redesign put the two things that matter above everything else: the catalogue and urgent delivery, side by side in the first screen. Gazprom Arena, Pulkovo and the Admiralty Shipyards sit under the fold as partners, because in B2B the client list argues better than any slogan. Everything else got out of the way.
